Spell Chess How it works Play

Chess, plus two spells.

Every turn you may cast one spell before you move. Spells are limited, they recharge after three turns, and they break the rules you thought you knew — including the one that says the king can't be taken.

Freeze

Five per game. Locks a 3×3 area for a turn — nothing inside can move, and frozen pieces stop giving check entirely. It catches your own pieces too.

Jump

Two per game. Makes one occupied square see-through for a turn, so rooks, bishops and queens slide straight past the piece standing there — for both players.

King capture

A spell can open an attack your opponent never had a chance to answer — so the king can simply be captured, and that wins the game on the spot.

    Engine

    The rules here are the same ones Spell Chess on chess.com uses — but chess.com only lets you play other people: no engine, no analysis. That's the gap this fills. Every move, spell and search runs in your browser against this repo's WebAssembly engine: the dock's cast buttons stage a spell exactly as turns reports it, and the committed move goes through apply.
